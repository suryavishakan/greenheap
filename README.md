# Greenheap Enterprises

Welcome to the official GitHub repository of **Greenheap Enterprises**, a diversified company operating across multiple sectors—**Foods**, **Gold Investments**, and **Real Estate**—with a mission to deliver value-driven products and services for a modern, growing India.

---

## 🏢 About Us

**Greenheap Enterprises** is the parent organization for three thriving business verticals:

- 🥘 **Greenheap Foods** – Offering authentic Indian masalas, spices, and ready-to-eat snacks, rooted in tradition and quality.
- 🪙 **Greenheap Gold** – A secure investment platform for purchasing and storing certified gold and precious metals.
- 🌱 **Cashback Farms** – Facilitating sustainable real estate and farmland ownership with transparent cashback models.

We aim to combine traditional values with modern business innovation to create meaningful customer experiences across industries.

## 🛠️ Tech Overview

Our technology stack varies across verticals, but commonly includes:

- **Frontend:** React.js, HTML/CSS, Tailwind  
- **Backend:** Node.js, Express, Python (Flask)  
- **Database:** MongoDB Atlas, PostgreSQL  
- **DevOps:** GitHub Actions, Vercel, AWS  
- **CMS & Automation:** Firebase, n8n, and Airtable integrations
